Game Flow

The rules in brief

1

Rank the groups

In each group, order the four teams according to your prediction. The top two teams automatically qualify for the round of 32.

2

Choose third-place teams

Pick eight third-place teams. The order of your picks sets their playoff bracket placement, so your first pick receives the best matchup.

3

Complete the playoffs

The playoff bracket is built from your group rankings. Choose a winner in every match, and in knockout matches you can also enter a score for points when the matchup matches the real fixture.

4

Earn bonus points

A correct group winner is worth 8 points, each correct semi-finalist is worth 12, each correct finalist is worth 18, and the correct champion is worth 30.

5

Score playoff matches

Playoff match scoring considers the winner, exact score, goal difference, total goals, and bonuses for harder predictions.

6

Save and compare

Your picks save automatically to your account. Submit when the form is ready, then follow your overall score and the high scores table against other players.
